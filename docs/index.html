How to use Fusion Maker

  1. Songs. Tap Choose videos and pick your karaoke videos. Use the arrows to put them in order.
  2. Trim. Tap Trim on a song. Play it, tap Start here and End here, then Done.
  3. Joins. Choose how one song changes to the next, for example a smooth blend.
  4. Finish. Choose Video or Sound only, tap Make my fusion and wait. Then tap Save to this phone.

While the fusion is being made, keep this page open and the screen on. A long fusion can take several minutes.

Works in Chrome on Android phones and in Safari on iPhones with iOS 26 or newer. Your videos never leave the phone.
